Advanced tools›

Auto assign balances each floor separately across the 3 owner parties and the builder, in the same proportions as the overall split (owner share ÷ 3 per party). This keeps every floor broadly equal, not just the building total. Whole shops can't be split, so small floors will only be approximately equal.

Simulate combinations to hit a target

Searches for whole-unit combinations that add up close to the target — shops can't be split, so an exact match isn't always possible. With spread on, the overall target still comes first: each floor gets a proportional share of the target as a guide, but a floor can land under its share if another floor compensates by going a little over, so the total stays close to target while allocation still stays broadly even across floors. "All units" can suggest taking a shop currently held by another party; applying it will reassign that shop away from them.
